Vellaramkunnu, Idukki Heritage

Rooted In The High Altitude Spice Gardens Of Kerala

ALPHONSA SPICES is based in Pathumury Road, Vellaramkunnu, Idukki—a region world-famous for producing the highest grade of green cardamom and black pepper due to its cool climate, rich soil, and misty altitude.

By eliminating middle-men and sourcing directly from local Idukki plantation farmers, we ensure our spices retain maximum essential oils, rich flavor, and natural aroma without any chemical polishing or artificial coloring.
